Field note 1 · The lab in one picture

One healthcare app, engineered from Azure-hosted to NVIDIA-served

Northstar Provider Operations (fictional) · two public editions (2025 vs 2026) of a health plan's Marketplace provider manual
The business problem

343 pages, two editions. Staff must find what actually changed: fax numbers, vendors, rules.

Wrong answers cost money. A stale fax delays prior auth; a lapsed license can suspend payment.

Look-alikes fool people and AI. 2025 addenda look new in 2026; other products sit beside Marketplace in the same tables.

Answer: under 50 words, cites edition + page, code proves the page supports it, a person decides.

The five traps every answer must survive (fixed regression anchors)
1 · Pharmacy faxThe plan moved pharmacy authorizations to a new processor with a new faxCatches: missing a real change
2 · NICU auth2026 sends NICU requests to a named outside vendorCatches: invented vendor
3 · AI voiceNew 2026 ban on AI-voice calls to the plan; clinician review was already requiredCatches: blending old + new rules
4 · Payer IDUnchanged across editions, while other products list different IDsCatches: product mix-up
5 · 15-day PCPSet by the Sept 1, 2025 addendum, not new in 2026Catches: false “new” claim
